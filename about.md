  <p>
    I’m a biomedical engineer by training and profession. By day, I work with a multidisciplinary team of extraordinary engineers/scientists at the Electrical Nerve Block group under the mentorship of Dr. <a href="https://www.metrohealth.org/center-for-rehabilitation-research/researchers/tina-vrabec">Tina Vrabec</a>. Our lab focuses on developing and testing waveforms and electrodes for delivering electrical nerve block to “control” the nervous system.<br><br>
