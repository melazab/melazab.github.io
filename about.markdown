# About Me

![](./assets/about/_site/mohamed_elazab_headshot.jpg)

I'm a biomedical engineer by training and profession. By day, I work with a multidisciplinary team of extraordinary engineers/scientists at the Electrical Nerve Block group at the MetroHealth Research Institute under the leadership of Dr. [Tina Vrabec](https://www.metrohealth.org/center-for-rehabilitation-research/researchers/tina-vrabec). Our lab focuses on developing and testing waveforms and electrodes for delivering electrical nerve block to "control" the nervous system.

Before joining the Electrical Nerve Block group, I worked at the Cleveland Clinic in the Urological Biomechanics lab with Drs. [Margot Damaser](https://www.lerner.ccf.org/biomedical-engineering/damaser) and [Steve Majerus](https://majeruslab.com) from August 2021 to December 2023.

In my free time, I independently study computer/software engineering concepts
